# Notebook Two editorial commission

**Status:** Accepted
**Effective:** July 28, 2026
**Entry:** “Open Models, Closed System?”

## Decision

The editor explicitly commissioned and published a second Season Zero Notebook
inquiry. This is the “continue” decision for the purpose of the product
workflow. It does **not** establish that the representative-reader test in the
July 23 Season Zero contract was conducted, completed, or passed.

The founding inquiry remains public and visible. Notebook Two leads the
homepage because it is the newest entry; the site remains a research notebook,
not a daily publication or generalized CMS.

## Scope

Notebook Two uses Xi Jinping’s July 2026 AI-governance proposal as its spine.
Typhoon Noul and the Fields Medals awarded to Hong Wang and Yu Deng are bounded
tests of state capacity, information quality, and transnational knowledge
networks. They are not treated as three independent investigations.

The commission authorizes:

- one Zod-validated Notebook registry;
- one static public route;
- one consent-gated audio player;
- one six-record “What to Watch” evidence register;
- the two-inquiry Archive relationship selector;
- first-party share links with UTMs; and
- source, methodology, SEO, and decision documentation.

It does not authorize new source intake, a transcript-ingestion pipeline,
analytics installation, a backend, a generalized CMS, a map, or a new
top-level product vertical.

## Release accounting

The release is recorded as three separate bodies of work so platform scope
does not stand in for editorial quality:

1. **Editorial research and authorship:** the source audit, listening review,
   claim decisions, 13-stop ledger, and authored inquiry.
2. **One-time Notebook platform work:** the validated two-entry registry,
   shared shell, Archive inquiry selection, sitemap, metadata, and documented
   URL contract.
3. **Optional interactive enhancement:** consent-gated audio and the
   URL-shareable “What to Watch” register.

The inquiry must remain understandable without loading audio or interacting
with the watch register. Those enhancements do not count as evidence that the
writing works.
