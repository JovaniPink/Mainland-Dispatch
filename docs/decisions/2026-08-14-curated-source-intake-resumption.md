# Curated source-intake resumption

**Status:** Accepted
**Effective:** August 14, 2026
**Scope:** One bounded, manually curated quality-link batch

## Decision

The editor directed Mainland Dispatch to continue building its quality-link
inventory from the past-week China search on HN Algolia. This decision narrowly
supersedes the Season Zero prohibition on additional article-intake batches.

It authorizes one manual intake review of the supplied discovery surface and the
addition of selected original-publisher URLs to the private source-lead catalog.
It does not authorize automatic scraping, recurring ingestion, public
publication, a generalized CMS, a new product area, or a change to any lead's
evidence status.

## Evidence boundary

HN popularity and comments are discovery signals only. They are not evidence
for a linked claim, are not retained as public destinations, and do not establish
source quality. Each selected lead points to the original publisher and remains
withheld until the canonical source, supporting records, counterpoints,
limitations and later developments pass the existing publication methodology.

The [August 14 intake ledger](../source-notes/2026-08-14-china-quality-links-review.md)
records the exact query, de-duplication, exclusions, endpoint checks, selected
URLs and review stopping points.

## Unchanged constraints

- The Desk and its source-lead inbox remain private.
- No selected link enters a public Dispatch, Notebook, Compare, Trace, Dossier,
  Atlas relation, sitemap or structured-data object.
- No HN discussion or popularity score becomes a source citation.
- Publication still requires lawful full-text review and the complete evidence
  gate for that specific source.
- Further intake batches require another explicit editorial decision.
