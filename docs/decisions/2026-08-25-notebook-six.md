# Inquiry 06 editorial commission

**Status:** Accepted

**Effective:** August 25, 2026

**Entry:** "What Gets Through?"

## Decision

Commission Inquiry 06 as a source-reviewed Season Zero Notebook about three
different gates on circulation: rules of origin for goods, networked attention
for culture, and national-security law for public memory.

The accepted thesis is comparative but not equivalizing. Customs authorities
classify origin and preference. Distributors, cinemas, platforms, and audiences
allocate attention. Prosecutors and courts apply coercive public law to
political advocacy. The comparison is useful because each gate has a subject,
gatekeeper, admission rule, observed movement, outcome, evidence status, and
caveat. It does not make tariff exposure, box-office visibility, and loss of
liberty morally equivalent.

## Evidence decision

The complete publisher audio for _China Insider_ episode 195 was audited, and
the trade, culture, and memory segments begin at 01:53, 20:48, and 29:38. The
official feed identifies an August 25, 2026 release and 39:23 runtime but
contains no transcript or chapter record. The public Notebook may use bounded
paraphrases and time locators, not quotations reconstructed from third-party
transcripts or the supplied synopsis.

The trade section may identify China's capped EV access to Canada, explain the
applicable origin-rule distinction, report the White House transshipment-risk
allegation, distinguish the separate Section 338 action, and present
Brookings' contrary aggregate reading. It may not claim that the reviewed
public record proves those EVs or other Chinese goods were transshipped into
the United States as Canadian-origin goods.

The culture section may report the dated AP and Maoyan-attributed reversal and
describe _Niu Lai_ as competing for attention or ranking visibility. It must
preserve daily, cumulative, admission, and opening-period denominators. It may
not convert incompatible snapshots into a same-day, same-period, or lifetime
revenue match with _The Odyssey_.

The memory section may report the convictions, the exact post-law
incitement-to-subversion theory, the judgment's treatment of "other unlawful
means," the defense position, the court's rejected and accepted theories, the
government position, and the rights counter-readings. It must distinguish the
Alliance's vigil history from the charged acts and the August 21 verdict from
mitigation and later sentencing.

## Product scope

This decision authorizes:

- one `circulation-gates` Notebook schema variant and exported
  `CirculationGatesNotebookEntry` type;
- one shared `NotebookAudio` type and click-to-load Simplecast audio contract
  with real loading, ready, error, retry, and unload states;
- exactly three uniquely named gate records: `trade`, `culture`, and `memory`;
- at least nine uniquely identified claim-audit records with complete source
  resolution;
- a three-source front-page synthesis that gives each gate one source;
- one static, responsive, semantic three-gate figure that requires no script or
  external request to be understood;
- one public route at `/notebook/what-gets-through` with shared reader chrome,
  canonical metadata, JSON-LD citations, source trail, and previous-public-
  inquiry link to Inquiry 04;
- registration as the latest public Notebook, with homepage, Notebook index,
  Archive selector, sitemap, and explicit test integration; and
- desktop and mobile review at 1440x900, 390x844, and 320-pixel widths in paper
  and night themes, including keyboard, source-anchor, overflow, and audio-
  privacy checks.

This decision does not authorize a map, private Desk exposure, promotion of
source leads, a new navigation area, a backend, a generalized ingestion or
transcription system, deployment, or a live-publication claim. Repository
publication requires a separately audited exact head and terminal hosted
checks.

## Review boundary

Research stops at the evidence, claim dispositions, counter-readings,
institutional context, and limitations recorded in the [Inquiry 06 source
ledger](../source-notes/2026-08-25-what-gets-through-ledger.md). Future updates
must date new box-office snapshots, distinguish a customs allegation from an
origin finding, read later Hong Kong procedural records before reporting them,
and rerun the repository and visual acceptance gates.

If a mandatory canonical gate fails, this decision does not authorize a
publication-complete claim. A webpack build may diagnose a known Turbopack
sandbox port failure, but it cannot replace the canonical repository gate.

## Rollback

Rollback removes Inquiry 06's registry entry, route, schema variant, content,
tests, and the two August 25 research documents. No existing public record,
private Desk item, or data migration is altered.
