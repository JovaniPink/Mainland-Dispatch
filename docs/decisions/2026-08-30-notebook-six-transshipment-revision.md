## Decision

Accept the August 30 transshipment research as a material revision to the
existing Inquiry 06. It does not commission Inquiry 08 or create a separate
Notebook, Dispatch, Compare, Trace, Dossier, or Atlas release.

The revision adds:

- a four-step proof path separating Canadian admission, Canadian production,
  USMCA origin qualification, and entry into the United States;
- a ten-event policy-pressure chronology;
- a five-position source matrix distinguishing publisher framing, official
  action, modeled risk, Canadian policy, and an independent counter-reading;
- expanded claim-audit records and source-specific limitations; and
- the static `TransshipmentEvidenceFigure` on the existing public route.

The [August 30 follow-up research
note](../source-notes/2026-08-30-us-canada-china-transshipment-follow-up.md)

## Accepted conclusion

China changed the policy, bargaining, and supply-chain environment surrounding
the US-Canada dispute. Canada's capped lower-tariff channel for China-origin
electric vehicles and US concerns about non-market investment, overcapacity,
origin rules, and transshipment sharpen the policy question.

The reviewed public record does not prove that Canada broadly laundered Chinese
origin, that a named firm or shipment evaded customs rules, or that EVs admitted
to Canada entered the United States as Canadian-origin goods. Policy concern,
modeled exposure, foreign production, tariff circumvention, USMCA qualification,
and adjudicated customs fraud remain different evidentiary objects.

## Relationship to future work

The trade material remains part of Inquiry 06 unless and until a separately
authorized, lossless migration creates Inquiry 09. The proposed ten-Notebook
program may study that split, but this revision does not authorize it.

Future evidence that could reopen the question includes:

- whether Canada's announced September 8 countertariffs took effect, changed,
  were suspended, or were replaced;
- issued import permits and actual use of the 49,000-vehicle quota;
- a Canada-specific customs determination, seizure, penalty, or
  origin-verification result;
- vehicle-level production and regional-value-content evidence;
- a material change in the USMCA review; or
- traceable Chinese manufacturing investment in Canada with production and
  origin records.

Those checks belong to the [transshipment evidence-watch
plan](../plans/2026-08-30-transshipment-evidence-watch.md). A new fact does not
automatically create a new Notebook; it triggers a dated editorial review.

## Rollback and correction

Rollback of the repository revision would remove the transshipment-specific
content and component while preserving both research ledgers. If a factual
problem is found after publication, Inquiry 06 remains public under the
correction policy with a dated `updatedAt` and `corrected` status rather than a
silent URL or evidence substitution.
