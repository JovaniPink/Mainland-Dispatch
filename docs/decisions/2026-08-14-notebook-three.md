# Notebook Three editorial commission

**Status:** Accepted
**Effective:** August 14, 2026
**Entry:** “Dominance Is a Dashboard, Not a Crown”

## Decision

The editor commissioned a third Season Zero Notebook around NPR’s August 12,
2026 _Fresh Air_ interview, “Has the U.S. lost its dominance to China?” The
Notebook is published as source-backed interpretation, not original reporting
and not a verified Dispatch record.

The interview and its related New Yorker feature supply the initiating question
and reported examples. They do not control the quantitative answer. Dashboard
values must come from identified official, intergovernmental, specialist, or
peer-reviewed records, retain their original denominator and date, and carry a
visible methodological boundary.

## Editorial model

“Dominance” is not treated as a measurable composite. The inquiry separates:

- manufacturing value added and concentrated production;
- nominal economic scale;
- research spending, publication output, biopharma programs, and AI capital;
- military expenditure;
- reserve-currency use;
- population scale, change, migration, and age structure; and
- the institutional history through which those positions developed.

Bars may compare the United States and China only inside a like-for-like metric.
The product must not add, average, rank, or normalize unlike indicators into a
winner. A longer bar in one card cannot be compared with a bar in another.

## Claim decisions

The broad industrial-lead claim is retained with disaggregated measures. The
episode’s blended “more than 70 percent” formulation is replaced by separate
IEA measures. The scientist-departure claim is narrowed to all destination
countries, not only China. The biopharma claim is narrowed to early-stage
programs and does not imply quality or approval. Xiaomi’s reported 76-second
factory cycle remains a qualified company vignette and is excluded from the
dashboard.

The agricultural before-and-after formulation, demographic determinism, and an
overall transfer of dominance are excluded from the public conclusion. Their
corrections remain in the claim-audit registry so the editorial decision is
testable without presenting the rejected language as a headline.

## Product scope

The commission authorizes:

- one `power-balance` Notebook schema variant with source-reference checks;
- one static public Notebook route;
- consent-gated NPR audio with the publisher transcript shown separately;
- paired-indicator, concentration, and demographic graphics rendered from the
  validated registry;
- a ten-stop historical timeline;
- the existing Archive, homepage, sitemap, sharing, metadata, and JSON-LD
  integrations; and
- source, methodology, and test documentation.

It does not authorize a live data pipeline, rankings, forecasting, analytics,
third-party graphics, original interviews, or promotion of prototype Dispatch
content into a public stream.

## Review boundary

Research stopped August 14, 2026 at the 23 public source stops recorded in the
[Notebook Three source ledger](../source-notes/2026-08-14-dominance-is-a-dashboard-ledger.md).
A future revision must preserve historical values, identify the later record,
advance the entry revision date, and update the ledger and tests. It must not
silently replace a dated observation with a newer one.
