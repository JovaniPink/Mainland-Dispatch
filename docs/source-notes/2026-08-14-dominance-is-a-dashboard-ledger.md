## Editorial question

NPR asked whether the United States has lost its dominance to China. This
review asks a prior methodological question: what kind of power is being
measured, in which unit, on what date, and with what capacity to affect an
outcome?

The answer is a non-additive dashboard. China’s manufacturing and selected
supply-chain leads, near-peer research scale, and demographic constraints are
displayed beside continuing US advantages in nominal GDP, measured private AI
investment, military expenditure, and reserve-currency use. No source reviewed
defines a valid conversion rate among those forms of power.

## Derived demographic display

China’s three bands are the NBS published categories: ages 0–15, 16–59, and
60-plus. For the United States, Mainland Dispatch downloaded the Census
resident population CSV `nc-est2025-agesex-res.csv`, selected the both-sexes
national rows, and summed single-year ages into the same display bands.

| Profile | Total | Age 0–15 | Age 16–59 | Age 60+ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| China, official NBS 2025 | 1.40489B | 16.4% | 60.6% | 23.0% |
| United States, derived from Census 2025 | 341,784,857 | 18.48% | 56.45% | 25.07% |

The public graphic rounds the US shares to 18.5%, 56.4%, and 25.1%. The three
bands sum to 100% within rounding. Alignment of display bands does not erase
differences in national population concepts, timing, migration, enumeration,
or methodology.

## Claims retained

- China leads the reviewed electric-car, battery-cell, solar supply-chain, and
  industrial-robot installation measures, with a different denominator and
  date shown for each.
- China’s manufacturing-value-added share is more than twice the US share in
  UNIDO’s 2024 estimate.
- China and the United States are at near-peer total R&D scale in NSF’s
  preliminary 2024 PPP comparison.
- China’s early-stage biopharma pipeline rose to near the US share by 2024.
- The United States retains much larger current-dollar GDP, measured private AI
  investment, military spending, and reserve-currency use in the cited series.
- China’s population declined in 2025 and its age structure creates a material
  long-run constraint.

## Claims qualified

- “More than 70 percent” is not published as a combined EV-battery-solar
  measure. The relevant IEA series are separated.
- Nearly 20,000 Chinese-origin scientists left the United States for all
  destination countries, not exclusively China.
- Almost one third describes early-stage biopharma programs, not promising
  compounds independently graded for quality or eventual approvals.
- Xiaomi’s 200 robots and 76-second cycle remain company-reported factory
  details. They are excluded from the comparative graphics.
- The IEA’s $6.5 trillion figure is explicitly a modeled exposure if controls
  were fully implemented, not realized damage.
- Private AI investment does not capture all Chinese public-guidance capital.

## Claims excluded from the public conclusion

- one blended “China share” across unlike industrial series;
- the imprecise shift from “more than three quarters agricultural” to “less
  than one quarter agricultural,” which changes possible units between
  population, residence, and employment;
- demographic aging as a deterministic countdown to Chinese decline;
- robot installations as equivalent to productivity or autonomous factories;
- expenditure as equivalent to military effectiveness;
- publication volume as equivalent to research quality; and
- a singular, dated transfer of global dominance from the United States to
  China.

The claim-audit registry retains the correction for each decision. Excluded
allegations are rendered publicly only as “Excluded overstatement,” not as
repeated headings.

## Graphic rules

- Paired bars compare only China and the United States inside one card.
- Bar lengths cannot be compared across cards with different units.
- Concentration measures remain separate and are never averaged.
- “More than” values are plotted at the stated floor and labeled as such.
- Every metric carries an as-of date, reading, caveat, and source reference.
- Demographic bars use aligned display bands with the national-method boundary
  visible beneath them.
- The page calculates no composite score, rank, probability, or forecast.

## Exact stopping point

The public-source review stopped August 14, 2026 after the 23 displayed source
stops. It did not:

- conduct original interviews or inspect a Chinese or American facility;
- verify private company production claims, classified capability, alliance
  decision-making, or intelligence assessments;
- model a Taiwan scenario, sanctions path, trade rupture, or future population;
- normalize national statistical systems beyond the disclosed US age-band sum;
- audit every field, patent, clinical program, model, mineral, weapon system,
  currency use, or supply-chain tier;
- establish causal credit for every historical shift; or
- convert unlike measures into an overall national-power score.

A later factual update must preserve the dated value in chronology, add the new
source and boundary, advance the entry revision date, and update tests. New
evidence must not silently overwrite a historical observation.
