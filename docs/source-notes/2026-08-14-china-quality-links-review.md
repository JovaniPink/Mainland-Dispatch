## Boundary

The query was captured on August 14 with a UTC cutoff after August 7. Two HN
records had no external URL. Eleven more records collapsed as repeated or
tracking-parameter variants, leaving 57 unique external URLs. Twenty were
selected for source breadth, publisher accountability, editorial relevance and
a direct path to evidence. The other 37 unique links were not imported because
they were off-topic keyword matches, social posts, weak or derivative sources,
or repeats of the same underlying story.

HN points, ranking, submitter identity and discussion data are intentionally not
retained in the catalog. Popularity can help surface a lead, but it is not an
evidence grade. No HN URL is a selected destination.

Endpoint checks resolved seven selected links directly on publisher pages. The
remaining thirteen stopped at subscription or automated-access controls: eight
paywalled sources and five restricted endpoints. A successful endpoint check
does not establish that the full article was read or that any claim was
verified.

## Next action

Resume one selected lead at a time. Obtain lawful full text, verify canonical
metadata, follow citations to primary records, build a minimal claim inventory,
add independent corroboration and serious counterpoints, and record later
developments and limitations. Only an evidence-reviewed lead may map one-to-one
to a Dispatch, and publication remains a separate gate.
