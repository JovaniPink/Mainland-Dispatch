## Editorial conclusion

The new Qingdao record advances the observed departure chronology by four days
and names the final Chinese port. It does not invalidate the commissioned
thesis or close any later proof gate. No reviewed record establishes arrival,
completed transit, reliable weekly service, safe passage, Suez-scale container
capacity, replacement of Hormuz energy flows, or lower lifecycle impact.

The existing NSIDC and IMO sources remain current enough for the September 1
publication cutoff. This refresh therefore authorizes no change to the closed
26-source, 31-reference, 28-URL, and 49-record custody inventory. The two later
context URLs remain attached to this dated addendum so a future review can
admit or retire them explicitly rather than silently changing public source
