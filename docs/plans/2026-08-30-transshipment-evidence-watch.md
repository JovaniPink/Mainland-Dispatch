## Source hierarchy

1. Operative customs, tariff, permit, court, treaty, or implementing records.
2. Responsible-government releases and datasets describing their own actions.
3. Firm or shipment records that can be tied to the legal test.
4. Independent analysis that preserves product, period, denominator, and legal
   distinction.
5. Publisher framing or discovery material, used only to locate the records
   above.

Restricted, redirected, or temporarily unavailable sources remain classified
under the link-audit policy. The watch never auto-replaces or removes a source.

## Editorial triggers

A dated review is required when one of the following becomes directly
inspectable:

- an operative Canadian countertariff record for September 8 or a replacement;
- official quota permits or utilization totals;
- a Canada-specific customs action or judicial record;
- vehicle-level origin documentation;
- operative USMCA text or implementation;
- operating Chinese-linked production in Canada with traceable inputs and
  origin treatment; or
- a correction to a controlling August 30 source.

The editor then decides among four outcomes: no verified change, a bounded
Inquiry 06 correction, a new dated source note, or a separate commission for
the proposed Inquiry 09. No outcome is automatic.

## Operating boundary

The watch is manual and read-only. It creates no scheduled workflow, scraping,
notification, ingestion, Desk promotion, public route, or publication promise.
GitHub tracks the review queue, while dated source notes remain the evidence
authority.

Close or supersede the watch only when a dated decision records why ongoing
monitoring no longer matters, a later source note replaces the question, or a
commission absorbs the remaining checks. Closing an issue is not evidence that
the underlying question was resolved.
